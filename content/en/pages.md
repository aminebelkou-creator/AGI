# Anglia Morocco — English examinations
Anglia Examinations is an English-language examination organisation based in England. Its official materials describe a ten-step General English pathway from First Step to Mastery (C2) and a separate four-level Business English pathway. General English exams assess listening, reading, writing and speaking; exact components vary by level.

Anglia's official franchise directory identifies AGI as its franchise holder for Morocco from 2021. Describe the local role according to the current agreement.

Anglia says its four highest General English qualifications are accredited through AIM Qualifications and Assessment Group and regulated by Ofqual. This does not apply automatically to every Anglia exam. Name the exact qualification, level and awarding body.

**Actions:** Explore levels · Find a centre · Ask about registration · Explore Step To books.

# C.I.T.E. — Certificate for International Teachers of English
C.I.T.E. is an Anglia Examinations assessment for people who teach English or are preparing to teach it. Anglia describes it as a test providing evidence of English-language competence relevant to teaching and mapped to the CEFR.

Anglia states that C.I.T.E. is not, by itself, a teaching qualification. Do not market it as a teaching licence, degree or standalone TEFL/TESOL award.

Anglia identifies three versions: C.I.T.E., C.I.T.E. (Young Learners) and C.I.T.E. (Very Young Learners). Check current specifications and recommended levels before publication.

**Action:** Ask Anglia Morocco about the current examination route.

# Teacher development
AGI's public materials refer to Anglia C.I.T.E., internships and partner-provided TEFL/TESOL learning. These are distinct offerings. C.I.T.E. is an Anglia examination assessing English competence relevant to teaching; Anglia says it is not itself a teaching qualification.

# Examination invigilation and proctoring
AGI's public materials describe invigilation for some paper-based and online examinations. Candidates must first confirm that their awarding institution accepts AGI as an off-campus venue and that the requested examination can be administered under its rules.

# Frequently Asked Questions
**Is AGI a school or a language centre?** AGI presents several educational activities. Its school activity, Anglia Morocco examination operation and other learning services are distinct.

**Does AGI follow an American curriculum?** AGI's public materials describe an American-curriculum framework. Ask for the currently approved educational project, authorised grades and delivery details.

**Are all Anglia exams Ofqual regulated?** No. Anglia says its four highest General English qualifications are accredited through AIM and regulated by Ofqual. This does not automatically apply to every exam.

**Is C.I.T.E. a teaching licence?** No. Anglia says C.I.T.E. is an examination providing evidence of English competence relevant to teaching; it is not itself a teaching qualification.

**Does AGI issue the adult diploma?** AGI's public materials refer to a pathway associated with AWA. Confirm the awarding provider and AGI's exact role for the current programme.

**Can AGI supervise an external exam?** AGI describes an invigilation service. Candidates must obtain their institution's approval and AGI's availability confirmation.

**How do I get current fees and dates?** Request the current dated schedule from the responsible team; do not rely on archived pages.
