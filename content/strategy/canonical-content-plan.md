# AGI canonical content plan — editorial master

**Date:** 17 November 2026  
**Status:** Proposed content plan for leadership/editorial review  
**Language order:** English meaning master → French (Morocco) → Arabic (Modern Standard Arabic, Morocco)

## Editorial objective

Make AGI understandable and credible to families, learners, teachers, professionals and institutional partners. The site should explain what AGI actually does, who delivers each offer, what evidence supports each claim, and what the visitor should do next.

The aim is not to make every page sound more promotional. It is to make the information more specific, useful, consistent and trustworthy.

## 1. The central narrative

### Proposed institutional description

**American Group International (AGI) is an education organisation based in Agadir, Morocco. Its activities bring together school education, inclusive learning support, English-language learning and examinations through Anglia Morocco, and selected learning and professional-development pathways.**

**Editorial note:** Proposed wording, not a legal description. Confirm the legal operator, school designation and actual current programme scope before publication.

### Brand promise

**A learning environment where people are understood, progress is made visible, and families know how to take the next step.**

This is a proposed promise, not a measurable guarantee. It should be supported by real examples of assessment, family communication, individual support and learner progress.

### Brand pillars

- **Accessible:** explain the real fees, conditions, options and support routes. Avoid unsupported “most affordable” claims.
- **Creative:** show teaching practices, learner projects, curiosity and participation. Do not rely on the adjective alone.
- **Inclusive:** explain the educational adjustments and family collaboration actually available. Do not imply clinical treatment or guaranteed outcomes.

### Motto

**School + Home + Community = Student Success.**

Use as a supporting message, not as a substitute for explaining how family and community involvement works.

## 2. Homepage editorial blueprint

### H1
**Education, language and opportunity — connected in Agadir.**

### Introductory copy
American Group International (AGI) brings together school education, inclusive learning support, English-language learning and assessment, and selected opportunities for adult learners, educators and professionals. We help visitors understand the right programme, the people responsible for it and the steps involved in moving forward.

### Audience routes
- **Families:** discover the school, learning approach, admissions and support for students.
- **English learners:** explore Anglia examinations, levels, preparation and local registration information.
- **Teachers:** understand C.I.T.E. as an Anglia examination and explore separate professional-development options.
- **Adult learners:** compare available study pathways and identify the organisation that awards each qualification.
- **Professionals:** enquire about English for workplace and sector-specific communication.
- **Education partners:** ask about examination centres and institutional collaboration.

### Trust section
**Clear information. A considered learning journey.**

Before choosing a programme, visitors should be able to find its eligibility, provider, learning format, costs, assessment, qualification issuer (if any) and contact route. AGI should publish these details only when current and approved.

### Conversion actions
- Families: **Book a school visit**
- English learners: **Ask about an Anglia level assessment**
- Teachers: **Ask about C.I.T.E.**
- Adult learners: **Request pathway information**
- Professionals: **Discuss a training need**
- Partners: **Enquire about collaboration**

Do not imply that any action is an online booking unless the relevant workflow is actually operational.

## 3. Institutional story page

### Purpose
Explain the organisation's identity and development without mixing the school's history with the history of Anglia Examinations or related legal entities.

### Proposed structure
1. What AGI is today.
2. How the organisation began (only after the founding date and first-cohort details are confirmed).
3. The school in Agadir: educational model and community.
4. The development of English-language and examination activities.
5. Anglia Morocco: separate franchise/operational relationship, with current approved wording.
6. Other programmes and providers, each described separately.
7. Leadership and team, with current titles and approved biographies.
8. Mission, vision and values, approved by leadership.

### Proposed mission draft
**To support learners through education, language development and inclusive opportunities, while building meaningful connections between families, educators and the wider community.**

### Proposed vision draft
**A learning community in which every learner can develop knowledge, confidence and the skills to participate in an interconnected world.**

Both are drafts for leadership review. Do not publish them as established institutional statements until approved.

## 4. School page

### Core question
What is the school offering in Agadir, which learners can apply, how does learning work, and what should families expect?

### Required content
- Legal school name and operator.
- Authorised levels and grade range.
- Curriculum and subjects by grade.
- Language of instruction and language support.
- Class organisation and teaching approach.
- Assessment and reporting.
- Inclusion and educational support.
- Student life and family communication.
- Admissions, placement and fees.
- Policies and safeguarding.

### Proposed opening
**A school journey built around the learner.**

AGI's school information should help families understand the educational programme, the learning environment and the support available at each stage. The school team can explain grade placement, curriculum, assessment and admissions during a family conversation or visit.

**Publication gate:** Replace broad descriptions with confirmed grade-by-grade information and the exact approved legal/authorisation wording.

## 5. Inclusive learning page

### Editorial boundary
Describe educational support, not diagnosis or treatment. Clearly separate services delivered by the school from services provided by external specialists.

### Proposed opening
**Understanding the learner is the starting point.**

Learners have different strengths, experiences and support needs. AGI's educational approach should explain how the school listens to families, reviews available learning information, agrees practical classroom strategies and reviews progress. Any specialist or clinical service must be identified separately, with its provider, qualifications, scope, availability and safeguarding arrangements.

### Required disclosures
- What support is educational and delivered by the school.
- What is external, specialist or clinical.
- Who provides each service and their credentials.
- Eligibility, availability, fees and referral process.
- Consent, privacy, safeguarding and emergency boundaries.

## 6. Anglia Morocco page

### Core editorial distinction
Anglia Examinations owns the examination framework and official qualification specifications. Anglia Morocco describes local delivery and franchise activity. AGI must describe its local role using the current agreement.

### Proposed opening
**English learning and examinations, step by step.**

Anglia Examinations offers English-language assessments across a range of levels. The Anglia Morocco section should help candidates understand the available examination routes, level descriptions, assessment components, preparation resources and local registration process.

### Page modules
- General English levels.
- Business English.
- C.I.T.E. examinations.
- Qualification and awarding-body information.
- Current examination dates and registration.
- Current examination centres.
- Step To books and resources.
- Candidate support and special arrangements.
- FAQs and contact.

### Mandatory qualification boundary
C.I.T.E. must be described as an examination/assessment, not a teaching licence or a standalone TEFL/TESOL qualification. AIM/Ofqual wording must name the exact qualifications covered. UCAS wording must refer only to the relevant Mastery qualification and be checked against current official information.

## 7. Adult and professional pathways

Every pathway page must identify:

- The intended learner and eligibility.
- The actual provider and awarding body.
- AGI's precise role (provider, partner, agent, tutor, referral or proctor).
- Learning format, duration and assessment.
- Credential issued and verification route.
- Recognition limitations and transfer disclaimers.
- Current price, payment terms and refund conditions.
- Contact person/team and next step.

Do not use “accredited”, “recognised”, “degree”, “diploma”, “university pathway” or “authorised agent” as generic marketing terms. Explain the exact scope and responsible organisation.

## 8. Journal and editorial content

### Keep as historical record
- Graduations and learner achievements.
- Institutional milestones and dated partnerships.
- Events and community activities.
- Examination and educational updates.
- Teacher development and resource announcements.

### Improve the article format
Each article should include:
- Accurate original publication date.
- Event date where different.
- Clear title and one-sentence summary.
- A substantive article body, not a short announcement expanded with invented facts.
- Named source/author where known.
- Correct entity names and partner role.
- Image captions and documented consent/rights.
- Related programme or resource link.
- Historical label when information is no longer current.

### New editorial series to develop (only with real source material)
1. **Inside the learning day:** teaching practices and learner projects.
2. **A parent's guide:** admissions, placement, assessment and school communication.
3. **English in practice:** useful language-learning guidance and exam preparation.
4. **Understanding qualifications:** explain awarding bodies, levels and recognition carefully.
5. **People who teach:** educator profiles and professional learning.
6. **Community and opportunity:** verified events, partnerships and student activities.

These are editorial concepts, not claims that such activities or interviews already exist.

## 9. French and Arabic localisation

- Translate the meaning, not the English syntax.
- Use the correct local terms for school fees, enrolment, grade levels, assessment and inclusive education.
- Explain US/UK education terms without implying automatic equivalence with Moroccan qualifications.
- Preserve official programme and qualification names.
- Keep Arabic paragraphs concise, natural and fully RTL.
- Do not translate legal or regulatory wording without review by a qualified specialist.

## 10. Editorial approval checklist

A page is ready for approval only when:
- Its audience and purpose are clear.
- The service or programme is currently delivered.
- Every material claim has an evidence reference.
- Fees and dates have an effective date and owner.
- Provider and awarding body are named correctly.
- Images, names and testimonials have appropriate consent.
- The English master is approved.
- French/Arabic are independently reviewed.
- The CTA leads to a real and working next step.
- The review date and owner are recorded.

**No publication approval is granted by this plan.**
