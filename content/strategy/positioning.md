# AGI Positioning and Messaging Architecture

## Recommended position
**American Group International is an education organisation in Agadir connecting school education, inclusive learning, English-language development and assessment, teacher-focused services and wider educational opportunities.**

French: **AGI est une organisation éducative à Agadir qui relie l’enseignement scolaire, l’accompagnement inclusif, l’apprentissage et l’évaluation de l’anglais, le développement professionnel des enseignants et différentes perspectives de formation.**

Arabic: **AGI منظمة تعليمية بأكادير تجمع بين التعليم المدرسي والمواكبة الدامجة وتعلم اللغة الإنجليزية وتقييم الكفايات فيها والتطوير المهني للمدرسين وآفاق تعليمية متنوعة.**

## Brand hierarchy
1. **American Group International (AGI):** institutional umbrella, story, mission, governance and navigation.
2. **AGI School — Agadir:** parent-facing school activity. Legal designation must match AREF authorisation.
3. **Anglia Examinations:** examination organisation and owner of official exam names/specifications.
4. **Anglia Morocco:** country-level franchise/partner operation. AGI is identified by Anglia as its Morocco franchise holder from 2021.
5. **Other activities:** Learning Pods, adult/AWA pathway, C.I.T.E., Bridge TEFL/TESOL, professional/Medical English, proctoring, wellbeing, university guidance and document editing. Each needs a distinct provider and relationship statement.

## Message pillars
**Affordable:** transparent, current fees and inclusions. No unsupported lowest-price claims.  
**Creative:** actual learning practices, projects, inquiry and participation.  
**Inclusive:** actual adjustments, individual planning, family collaboration and referral arrangements.

Supporting motto: **School + Home + Community = Student Success.**

## Homepage sequence
1. Explain AGI and Agadir.
2. Route parents clearly to the school.
3. Route English learners, teachers, professionals, adults and institutions to distinct services.
4. Explain Affordable / Creative / Inclusive with real examples.
5. Tell founder and institutional story.
6. Introduce Anglia Morocco separately.
7. Present verified credentials and partners.
8. End with visit, admissions or programme enquiry.

## Voice
- English master: warm, confident, plain US English; use “program”, “enrollment”, “individualized”.
- French: natural Moroccan institutional French; use “programme”, “frais de scolarité”, “évaluation de positionnement”, “accompagnement pédagogique”.
- Arabic: Modern Standard Arabic, contemporary and readable; use “التربية الدامجة”, “المواكبة التربوية الفردية”, “اختبار تحديد المستوى”, “ولي الأمر”.
- Keep official product names and qualification titles unchanged.
