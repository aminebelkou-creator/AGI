# CMS-Independent Information Architecture

## Navigation
- Home
- About AGI: Our story; Mission and values; Leadership; Educational approach; Partnerships
- Learning at AGI: School; Inclusive learning; Learning Pods/tutoring; Adult pathways
- English and Examinations: Anglia Morocco; General English; Business English; C.I.T.E.; Step To books; Centres; Exam arrangements
- Professional Development: Teacher development; Bridge TEFL/TESOL (if confirmed); Professional English; Medical English
- Services: Proctoring; English document editing; Wellbeing (after scope verification); University guidance (after partner verification)
- Admissions and Family Services: Admissions; Fees; Policies; Forms and requests; Parent resources
- News and Resources
- Contact / Book a visit

Keep investor and institutional partnership information separate from parent-facing navigation.

## Reusable page anatomy
Title and summary; audience/problem; programme/service explanation; who it is for; inclusions; eligibility; provider/awarder/partner; delivery and location; fees/dates linked to current data; evidence; FAQs; primary action; related content.

## Journeys
**Parent:** Search/referral → school → curriculum/inclusion → admissions/fees → visit → assessment → offer/contract → enrolment → family services.  
**English candidate:** English → placement → CEFR/Anglia levels → preparation/books → centre/date → registration → exam → results/certificate.  
**Teacher:** teacher development → C.I.T.E. explanation → distinguish C.I.T.E. from TEFL/TESOL → eligibility → official registration.  
**Adult:** pathway → provider identity → accreditation/awarding evidence → eligibility → fees/mode → assessment → diploma/transcript → recognition limits.  
**Institution:** partnership → centre requirements → agreement/approval → candidate processes → quality assurance.

## Platform-neutral model
Pages are compositions of referenced content entities and reusable blocks. A CMS is only an editing/publishing interface. The same content should export to Markdown, JSON, YAML, REST, GraphQL, static-site generators, mobile apps and AI retrieval systems.
