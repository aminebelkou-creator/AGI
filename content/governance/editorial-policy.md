# Editorial, Evidence and Approval Policy

## Editorial status
Use: `draft`, `subject_review`, `legal_review`, `partner_approval`, `approved`, `scheduled`, `published`, `expired`, `withdrawn`. Claim-level status overrides page-level approval.

## Metadata required on each content entity
Stable ID; content type; title; slug; locale; source locale; translation relationship; owner; reviewer(s); status; related entities; claim IDs; source references; media and consent references; SEO title/description; created/updated dates; effective date and review/expiry date for dynamic content.

## Evidence rules
Every factual/comparative claim needs an exact approved wording, evidence source, scope/jurisdiction, effective date, expiry/review date, approving owner and prohibited paraphrases. A partner's public page does not replace a current contract when contract scope matters.

## Three-language workflow
1. Approve the English master meaning.
2. Verify facts and claim permissions.
3. Localise French and Arabic independently.
4. Review educational/legal terms with a subject-matter reviewer.
5. Confirm proper names and qualification titles remain unchanged.
6. Review Arabic RTL, numerals, punctuation and accessibility.
7. Publish locale variants with shared entity IDs.
8. Reopen translations when the underlying fact changes.

## Child safeguarding and privacy
No identifiable student image, name, video, case study or testimonial without specific documented consent from the appropriate legal representative and, where appropriate, the learner. Consent must identify purpose, channels, duration and withdrawal route. Do not use sensitive disability, health or family information as marketing material. Consent must be separate from enrolment consent.

## High-risk content requiring subject/legal/partner review
School legal designation and authorisation; foreign curriculum; diplomas and credits; accreditation/recognition; medical/psychological/therapeutic services; teacher licensing; university admission and visas; fees/contracts/refunds; privacy and student records.

## Dynamic data
Fees, dates, schedules, staff roles, contact details, availability and examination-centre lists must be stored as dated records with owner, effective date, expiry/review date and publication status. Do not hardcode them into evergreen copy.

## AI and retrieval
AI assistants may use only approved, current content. They must distinguish AGI from external providers and awarding bodies, retrieve claim status/effective dates, answer in the requested language, avoid guessing, cite sources, and escalate eligibility, recognition, disability support, clinical, fee or legal questions to a human.

## Corrections
Expire/withdraw the claim, remove it from all channels, identify translated/dependent pages, correct public content where needed, preserve audit history and notify the owner/partner.
