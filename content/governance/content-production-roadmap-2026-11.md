**Date:** 17 November 2026  
**Branch:** `cms-test-smartsite`  
**Status:** Working editorial plan — not a publication approval

## Editorial quality gates

Every page must have:
1. One defined audience and one primary purpose.
2. A clear title, concise opening and logical subheadings.
3. Accurate description of what is offered and who delivers it.
4. A specific CTA linked to a working contact or form.
5. SEO title and meta description.
6. Source references for material claims.
7. Publication status, owner and last-reviewed date.
8. Rights and consent checks for images, testimonials and third-party materials.
9. English master approval before Arabic/French localisation.
10. A route decision that preserves the old URL until an approved redirect is tested.

## Priority queue

### Priority A — resolve before launch
- Legal identity, institutional description and scope.
- School name, authorised levels, curriculum and current operating model.
- Admissions process, current fees, refund/complaint policy and academic calendar.
- Anglia relationship and local operating scope.
- Anglia exam levels, AIM/Ofqual wording, C.I.T.E. description, UCAS wording.
- Current Anglia exam sessions, prices, online availability and centres.
- Inclusive-learning service boundaries, staff credentials and safeguarding.
- Adult diploma awarding body, recognition and AGI's role.
- Privacy, cookies, student image consent and form data handling.

### Priority B — complete core experience
- Student life and family resources.
- Business English and teacher development.
- Proctoring and exam arrangements.
- Step To books, sample materials and learning resources.
- FAQs, contact routes, forms and internal links.
- Journal classification and historical dates.

### Priority C — strengthen and extend
- Editorial series and educational articles.
- Case studies and testimonials with consent.
- Downloadable guides and parent information.
- Additional sector-specific English content, only when programmes are confirmed.
- Structured content for future CMS import after approvals.

## Explicit holds

The following must not be promoted as current until verified:
- Current prices, calendars, exam dates and class schedules.
- The “12 regions” centre network and named representatives.
- Online/home examination delivery in Morocco.
- Clinical, counselling, occupational therapy, speech-language and behavioural services.
- Any C.I.T.E. claim that implies a teaching licence or standalone teaching qualification.
- Adult diploma recognition, accreditation or awarding arrangements.
- Teacher sponsorship, employment, visa or immigration pathways.
- Partner status, university agent authorisations, logos and testimonials.
- Current student counts, nationalities, staff titles and school superlatives.
