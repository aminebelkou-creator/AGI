# Anglia Morocco — source-to-content reconciliation

**Branch:** `cms-test-smartsite`  
**Date:** 17 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Reconcile legacy AngliaMorocco.org content and Anglia-related content hosted on the AGI website into one editorial structure, without flattening the Anglia Morocco identity.

## Scope and method

This inventory is generated from the repository's existing `docs/content-map.csv`. It includes every record whose source site is Anglia or whose title, URL, destination or section identifies Anglia, C.I.T.E., CEFR, Learning Pods or proctoring. It is a migration-planning aid, not a fresh crawl and not a claim that every source page is currently live or accurate.

The companion CSV records the source site, language, content type, legacy URL, proposed destination group, preliminary editorial action, priority and evidence gate. All actions are provisional.

## Canonical editorial groups

1. **Anglia Morocco — brand overview:** local identity, relationship with AGI, team and verified operating scope.
2. **General English examinations:** ten-step pathway, exam levels, skills assessed, candidate progression.
3. **Business English examinations:** four-step pathway, professional use cases and current level mapping.
4. **C.I.T.E. and teacher development:** one canonical page with precise provider/award/assessment description; do not market it as a teaching licence or standalone teaching qualification.
5. **CEFR and level guidance:** official Anglia mapping separated from AGI's own school benchmarks.
6. **Exam delivery and candidate access:** paper/online formats only where currently offered; special arrangements with a clear candidate request process.
7. **Exam dates and registration:** dated operational information with owner and last-updated date.
8. **Preparation resources:** Step To books, practice papers, audio and placement resources, with rights and availability checked.
9. **Exam centres and network:** only currently active, authorised and consented centres/representatives.
10. **FAQs and contact:** one verified answer base and unified contact routing.
11. **News and history:** dated archive, never presented as current service information.
12. **Site-wide legal information:** one coherent policy set after legal review of data controllers, forms, cookies and any Anglia-specific processing.

## High-risk items to resolve before publication

- Accreditation, regulatory status, awarding-body and recognition statements must be scoped to the exact qualification and checked against current primary records.
- Claims about UCAS tariff points, Ofqual, AIM, CCGi and any official partnership must be verified and worded precisely.
- C.I.T.E. content has inconsistent legacy descriptions; do not imply a teaching licence or standalone professional teaching qualification.
- Old exam calendars, Saturday schedules, pricing, online exam availability and centre lists must be treated as expired/unverified until reconfirmed.
- Special arrangements must describe the actual request process and available accommodations without implying clinical services.
- Practice papers, audio, Step To materials and images require rights/permission checks.
- Arabic and English legacy pages should be reconciled as language counterparts, not automatically counted as separate services.
- Preserve every legacy URL until route-by-route redirect decisions are approved and tested.

## Decisions intentionally not made

This inventory does not approve deletion, redirects, publication, new service claims, current prices, exam dates, centre status, partner logos, staff titles or legal wording. The old domains and routes remain untouched.

## Recommended sequence

1. Approve the canonical Anglia Morocco content groups.
2. Verify exam and qualification claims against Anglia Examinations and the relevant awarding-body primary sources.
3. Confirm local operating facts with the Anglia Morocco content owner.
4. Consolidate the English master copy.
5. Localise into Arabic and French after the English source is approved.
6. Map legacy routes to canonical pages and prepare a separate redirect plan for approval.
