# AGI live website content and publication-risk audit

**Version:** 1.0  
**Review date:** 16 November 2026  
**Scope:** Public-facing AGI and Anglia Morocco websites; content, information architecture, consistency, trust and publication-risk review.  
**Status:** Desk review only. No CMS, hosting, DNS, analytics or source-code access was used. No website changes were made.

## Executive assessment

The public website already contains a broad range of material and useful operational information. The main weakness is not a lack of content; it is the absence of a single controlled source of truth. School admissions, Anglia language courses and exams, adult/online pathways, inclusion, wellbeing, proctoring and partner services appear under one large navigation system. This creates audience confusion and makes it difficult to distinguish AGI-delivered services from partner-awarded credentials or external providers.

The immediate priority should be a **publication freeze and factual refresh** for time-sensitive and high-risk claims—not a cosmetic redesign. In particular, the public fee pages, academic calendar, qualification/accreditation statements, health/wellbeing descriptions, school status and refund rules need owner confirmation before reuse in new English, French or Arabic pages.

## Findings and required action

### P0 — Resolve before publishing new copy

1. **School legal identity and authorisation.** Reconcile the legal operator in Morocco, AREF authorisation, approved school name and grades, approved educational project/curriculum, and any authorisation/specifications applicable to foreign education under Article 58 of Law 59.21. Public website statements do not prove regulatory status. Obtain written confirmation from AREF Souss-Massa and Moroccan counsel.
2. **Fees and contractual transparency.** Confirm the current 2026–27 fee schedule, what each amount covers, all ancillary fees, insurance, books/materials, exam fees, payment dates, refunds, and the responsible contracting entity. Law 59.21 Articles 49 and 50 are particularly relevant. This audit is not a legal opinion.
3. **Credential and accreditation language.** For each pathway, identify the awarding/accrediting body, exact programme, legal entity, covered grades, validity period, territory, and AGI's role. Publish only after documentary verification.
4. **Therapy, counselling and health claims.** Separate educational accommodations and pastoral support from clinical diagnosis, psychotherapy and healthcare. Verify provider qualifications, scope of practice, professional registration where required, safeguarding, consent, referrals, emergency procedures, record handling and liability.
5. **Refund policy consistency.** Confirm how policies interact by product and contracting entity, ensure consistency with applicable law and partner terms, and publish one controlled policy with a clear effective date.

### P1 — Correct promptly

6. **Outdated school calendar.** The page title says “Academic School Calendar 2026–2027”, but search-visible content begins with “First Day of Classes – September 8th, 2025”. Replace with the approved 2026–27 calendar or remove until corrected.
7. **Outdated language-course pricing.** The Anglia pricing page states prices are effective November 2024. Treat amounts, class sizes, course duration, exam inclusions and schedule statements as historical until the programme owner confirms current terms.
8. **Inconsistent naming and hierarchy.** Public copy alternates among AGI, AGIC, American School of Agadir, Anglia Morocco Language Center and other variants. Create an approved legal-name and brand-architecture block for each activity.
9. **Claims that need evidence.** “First American school in Agadir”, “46+ nationalities”, “lowest possible” fees and placement outcomes need dated evidence, a defined measurement period and approval. Remove unsupported claims until documented.
10. **Audience and menu overload.** Reorganise by audience and service line. Put legal/policy documents in a clearly labelled resource centre rather than exposing every policy as top-level navigation.

### P2 — Improve for conversion and maintainability

11. Separate the parent-focused school journey from the Anglia examination journey.
12. For partner pathways, explain who awards, teaches, collects payment, supports the learner and governs the service.
13. Use one primary conversion action per audience and assign a monitored owner to each enquiry route.
14. Review privacy and consent for children’s data, health-related information, photographs, testimonials and partner transfers.
15. Translate only approved source content; keep dynamic facts in shared structured records.

## Recommended content architecture

1. **AGI** — organisation, approach, location, leadership, safeguarding, contact.
2. **School in Agadir** — school model, approved grades, curriculum, learning support, admissions, fees, calendar, parent resources.
3. **Anglia Morocco** — examination board overview, Morocco centre/network, exams, candidate support and exact C.I.T.E. scope.
4. **Learning and professional services** — language classes, adult learning, proctoring, teacher development, learning pods.
5. **Partner pathways** — one page per currently active and documented pathway, identifying the awarding body and AGI's role.
6. **Student wellbeing and inclusion** — only after scope, credentials, safeguarding and referral pathways are verified.
7. **Resources** — policies, forms, calendar, fee schedules, FAQs and notices, each with version/effective date.

## Minimum evidence pack required from AGI

- Moroccan legal operator identity and current company/association records relevant to each activity.
- AREF authorisations, approved school name and grade scope, approved educational project/curriculum, and written position on Article 58.
- Current 2026–27 fee schedule, enrolment contract, insurance policy, refund/complaints policy and academic calendar.
- Programme register showing active/paused/discontinued services, owner, location, format, age range, fees and dates.
- Anglia franchise/centre documentation, current exam schedule, candidate regulations, fee list and exact accreditation evidence.
- Current agreements and scope statements for AWA, CCG/CCGi, Bridge, Leverage Edu and other providers.
- Evidence for accreditation, recognition, “first” and nationality/student-outcome claims.
- Staff biographies and qualifications; wellbeing provider credentials; safeguarding and referral procedures.
- Privacy notice, consent mechanisms, child-data practices, image/testimonial permissions and retention schedule.

## Suggested 10-working-day remediation sequence

- **Days 1–2:** Freeze unverified claims and expired dates/prices; appoint content owners and approvers.
- **Days 3–4:** Collect legal, AREF, fee, calendar, partner and credential evidence.
- **Days 5–6:** Approve brand architecture, navigation and audience journeys.
- **Days 7–8:** Rewrite high-risk pages in English master; approve facts before translation.
- **Days 9–10:** Adapt French and Arabic, run consistency checks, test enquiry routes and publish only approved pages.

## Limitations

This is a public-web desk audit, not a legal opinion, accreditation verification, accessibility audit, technical SEO crawl, security assessment, analytics review or inspection of AGI's internal records. Website content is treated as a statement made by the publisher, not independent proof. Resolve discrepancies against current signed documents and written authority/partner confirmation.
