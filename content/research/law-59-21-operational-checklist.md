# Law 59.21 — AGI Operational and Website Checklist

**Working legal research, not legal advice.** Read the law in Arabic as published in the Official Bulletin. Verify the promulgated official bulletin version before legal reliance.

## Relevant provisions

- **Article 42:** legality, transparency, integrity, equality of access, continuity and communication with users.
- **Article 43:** authorisation from the relevant AREF for opening, expansion, changes and adding cycles/programmes; preliminary approval is not final authorisation.
- **Article 44:** private-school advertising may be issued after preliminary AREF approval and must not mislead learners or parents about authorised cycles, programmes, qualifications or services.
- **Article 45:** the proposed school designation must correspond to level/cycles/category and include the statutory private-school wording.
- **Article 46:** minimum requirements for premises, equipment, supervision, guidance, programmes and curricula.
- **Article 48:** the educational project and curriculum/programme reference list must be submitted to AREF for approval.
- **Article 49:** a published and permanently available fee schedule including annual registration, insurance, monthly tuition and applicable ancillary services; no fee increases during the current school year; advance notice of possible next-year increases.
- **Article 50:** written contract with the learner's legal representatives defining each party's rights and obligations; copy to the representatives.
- **Article 51:** no compulsory purchase of books, educational aids or supplies from the school and no direction to a specific bookstore.
- **Article 53:** regular educational, administrative and health inspections by AREF; inspection is not accreditation.
- **Articles 54–55:** staff pedagogical qualifications and continuing professional development provisions.
- **Article 58:** foreign education may be provided by a private school only with authorisation under conditions set by a specifications document approved by regulatory text; authorised institutions must meet specified national-identity, Arabic/Amazigh and other obligations.

## AGI verification checklist

### Legal entity and school authorisation
- [ ] Identify the legal owner/operator of the Agadir school.
- [ ] Obtain current AREF opening authorisation and every amendment.
- [ ] Confirm the approved designation and wording.
- [ ] Confirm site address and approved capacity/premises.
- [ ] Confirm each authorised cycle/grade and programme.
- [ ] Obtain Article 58 foreign-education authorisation and applicable specifications, if AGI provides foreign education.
- [ ] Obtain approved educational project and curriculum reference list.
- [ ] Confirm Arabic and Amazigh requirements and national-identity programme.
- [ ] Confirm current school year and official school records.

### Fees and enrolment
- [ ] Publish a single dated fee schedule, aligned with Article 49.
- [ ] Separate annual registration, insurance, tuition and optional services.
- [ ] Confirm whether tuition includes all educational activities.
- [ ] Review fee-change process and parent notification.
- [ ] Have Moroccan counsel review the written enrolment contract and current implementing texts.
- [ ] Ensure families receive a copy of the signed contract.
- [ ] Make books and supplies optional; do not direct families to a specific seller.

### Marketing and content
- [ ] Remove “first”, “accredited”, “recognised”, “international school” and “K–12” claims unless approved and evidenced.
- [ ] Ensure advertisements match approved grade/programme scope.
- [ ] Distinguish the Moroccan school from Anglia exam services and third-party providers.
- [ ] Ensure partner claims and logos are contractually approved.
- [ ] Keep old dates and fee pages out of search results or clearly mark archived.
- [ ] Ensure translations carry the same legal meaning.

## Regulatory uncertainty to resolve

Article 58 refers to conditions to be defined in a specifications document approved by regulatory text. This desk review does not establish whether all implementing texts have been issued or how they apply to AGI. Obtain written advice from AREF Souss-Massa and Moroccan counsel. Do not infer that a foreign curriculum is authorised merely because it is delivered by a US-registered company or associated with a foreign partner.
