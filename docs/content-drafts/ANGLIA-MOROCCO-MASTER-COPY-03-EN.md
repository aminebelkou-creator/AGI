## 2. General English examinations

**SEO title:** Anglia General English Examinations | Ten-Step Pathway  
**Meta description:** Discover Anglia's ten-step General English examination pathway, from First Step to Mastery, and explore the skills assessed at each level.

# General English examinations: progress step by step
Anglia's General English examination pathway is designed to assess English language ability across a progressive series of levels. It begins with early-stage learning and continues through to advanced proficiency.

The examinations assess language skills including listening, reading, writing and speaking. The exact components and optional elements depend on the examination level. Consult the official specification for the level you intend to take.

### The ten-step pathway
1. **First Step** — an introductory level for learners beginning to use English.
2. **Junior** — develops basic vocabulary, useful expressions and simple communication.
3. **Primary** — extends vocabulary and the ability to ask, answer and describe.
4. **Preliminary** — develops more independent use of English and introduces more distinct skill assessment.
5. **Elementary (A2)** — supports communication in familiar, everyday situations.
6. **Pre-Intermediate (A2+)** — extends language range and confidence beyond elementary use.
7. **Intermediate (B1)** — assesses independent communication at the B1 level.
8. **Advanced (B2)** — assesses more confident and effective communication at B2.
9. **Proficiency (C1)** — assesses advanced language ability at C1.
10. **Mastery (C2)** — the highest level in the General English pathway, mapped to C2.

The first four stages are presented as learning steps rather than direct CEFR equivalents. The CEFR mapping must be read from Anglia's current official level information; it is not appropriate to assume that every step has an exact one-to-one CEFR equivalence.

### Which level should I choose?
The right examination depends on your current language ability, previous learning and purpose. Review the level descriptions and sample papers, and ask the local team about the current placement or guidance process. A placement activity should not be described as an official Anglia examination unless Anglia confirms that status.

**CTA:** Ask for level guidance  
**Secondary CTA:** View official Anglia level information

## 3. Business English examinations

**SEO title:** Anglia Business English Examinations in Morocco  
**Meta description:** Explore four Anglia Business English examination levels, from A2+ to C1, focused on communication in professional contexts.

# Business English examinations
Anglia Business English examinations assess English communication in practical business and workplace contexts. They are intended for learners who want to evaluate their ability to use English in professional situations.

The official pathway includes four levels:
- **Practical Business English — A2+**
- **Intermediate Business English — B1**
- **Advanced Business English — B2**
- **Proficiency in Business English — C1**

The examinations focus on practical communication and assess reading, writing, listening and speaking. Tasks may involve workplace exchanges, business correspondence, interpreting information and responding to professional situations. Candidates should consult the current specification for the exact task types at each level.

### Who may find these examinations useful?
- Students preparing for work in an international environment.
- Professionals who use English in workplace communication.
- Learners who want a structured way to assess business-related English ability.

An examination result is evidence of performance in the examination; it is not a guarantee of employment, promotion or admission.

## 4. C.I.T.E. examinations for teachers of English

**SEO title:** Anglia C.I.T.E. Examinations for Teachers of English  
**Meta description:** Learn about Anglia's C.I.T.E. examinations, which assess English-language competence in teaching-related contexts.

# C.I.T.E. examinations: English competence for teaching contexts
C.I.T.E. stands for Certificate for International Teachers of English. It is an Anglia examination designed to assess relevant English-language competence in teaching-related contexts.

The official Anglia description is explicit: **C.I.T.E. is not itself a teaching qualification.** It recognises competence in the English language skills relevant to teaching; it should not be presented as a teaching licence, a standalone TEFL/TESOL qualification or a substitute for teacher education, professional registration or any legally required credential.

### Who may consider C.I.T.E.?
The examination may be relevant to current teachers of English and to people preparing for teaching contexts who have developed the necessary classroom-language knowledge and practice. Anglia describes versions for different learner-age contexts:
- C.I.T.E.
- C.I.T.E. (Young Learners)
- C.I.T.E. (Very Young Learners)

### Choosing a version
The English-language level and test content differ by version. Anglia's official guidance indicates different expected language levels for the three versions. Candidates should review the current test specifications and sample papers before choosing.

### Preparation
Preparation should be based on the official overview, test specifications and sample papers. Anglia states that there is no set course that automatically leads to the C.I.T.E. test. Any local preparation support offered by Anglia Morocco must be described separately and only if currently available.

**CTA:** Ask about C.I.T.E. examination availability  
**Secondary CTA:** Review official C.I.T.E. specifications

## 5. Understanding CEFR and Anglia levels

**SEO title:** Understanding CEFR Levels and Anglia Examinations  
**Meta description:** Understand the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages and how Anglia examination levels are mapped to it.

# Understanding CEFR levels
The Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) is a framework used to describe language proficiency. It provides a shared reference for discussing what learners can do with a language.

The six broad CEFR levels are:
- **A1 — Beginner**
- **A2 — Elementary**
- **B1 — Intermediate**
- **B2 — Upper intermediate**
- **C1 — Advanced**
- **C2 — Proficient user**

Anglia maps specified examination levels to CEFR levels to help candidates understand the broad proficiency level being assessed. A mapping is a reference, not a claim that two different examinations are identical in content, format or assessment.

### Anglia's published mappings
The official Anglia General English information maps Elementary to A2, Pre-Intermediate to A2+, Intermediate to B1, Advanced to B2, Proficiency to C1 and Mastery to C2. The first four steps should be described using Anglia's own level descriptions rather than assigning unsupported exact CEFR equivalents.

**Important:** AGI school learning expectations, internal benchmarks or grade-level targets are not official Anglia examination mappings. Keep these concepts separate.

**CTA:** Explore General English levels

**Evidence gate:** Check the live Anglia mapping and CEFR wording before each publication cycle.

## 10. Frequently asked questions

### Are Anglia General English examinations available at different levels?
Yes. Anglia publishes a ten-step General English pathway from First Step to Mastery. The availability of each level in Morocco must be confirmed for the relevant session.

### Are all Anglia examinations Ofqual-regulated?
No. The official information identifies specific higher-level General English qualifications delivered with AIM Qualifications and Assessment Group. Do not generalise that status to every Anglia examination or to the Business English and C.I.T.E. tests.

### What is the CEFR?
The CEFR is a framework for describing language proficiency. Anglia maps specified examination levels to CEFR levels; the mapping does not mean different examinations are identical.

### Is C.I.T.E. a teaching licence?
No. Anglia states that C.I.T.E. is not itself a teaching qualification. It is an examination recognising relevant English-language competence in teaching contexts.

### Does Mastery provide UCAS tariff points?
Anglia's official information states that its Mastery qualification carries UCAS tariff points. Candidates should check the current official Anglia and UCAS information and the precise qualification before relying on this for an application.

### Can I take an examination online?
Delivery options depend on the examination, session and local arrangements. Contact Anglia Morocco to confirm current availability.

### How much does an examination cost?
Fees may change. Request the current fee for the selected level and session.

### Where can I find preparation materials?
Anglia publishes Step To books and other preparation resources. Ask the local team about current availability and permitted access.

### Can I request examination arrangements?
Contact the team before registration or as early as possible. The available arrangements and evidence requirements depend on the examination provider's rules.
