## 1. Inclusive learning at AGI

**SEO title:** Inclusive Learning and Educational Support | AGI  
**Meta description:** Learn about AGI's approach to inclusive learning, individual educational needs and communication with families.

# Learning should make room for every learner
Learners bring different strengths, interests, experiences and support needs to the classroom. An inclusive educational approach seeks to reduce barriers to participation and help each learner engage meaningfully in learning.

At AGI, the description of inclusive learning should focus on the educational support that the school can actually provide, how families and educators communicate, and how learning needs are reviewed.

### A considered approach
- **Understand the learner:** listen to the family and learner, review relevant educational information and clarify learning priorities.
- **Plan appropriate support:** identify reasonable classroom strategies and learning adjustments within the school's approved capacity.
- **Work with families:** agree on communication channels, review points and shared expectations.
- **Review progress:** discuss what is working and what may need to change.

### Important distinction
Educational support is not the same as clinical diagnosis, therapy or medical treatment. Do not advertise occupational therapy, speech-language pathology, counselling, behavioural therapy or other clinical services unless the provider, credentials, scope, safeguarding arrangements, referral pathway and current availability have been independently verified.

**CTA:** Discuss educational support needs

**Approval gates:** Confirm the actual service model, age/grade range, staff roles and credentials, referral procedure, safeguarding, data handling, consent and capacity. Specialist and legal review is mandatory for any clinical or therapeutic service description.

## 2. Student life

**SEO title:** Student Life and School Community | AGI  
**Meta description:** Discover how learning, relationships and school activities contribute to the student experience at AGI.

# Learning beyond the classroom
A school experience is shaped by more than lessons. Relationships, routines, projects and shared activities can help learners develop confidence, responsibility and a sense of belonging.

This page should introduce the activities and experiences that AGI can verify for the current academic year. It may include:
- classroom projects and collaborative learning;
- school events and celebrations;
- clubs, workshops or enrichment activities, where currently offered;
- student participation and community initiatives;
- guidance for families on attendance, communication and school routines.

### A shared school community
Students, families and educators each contribute to a positive learning environment. AGI aims to communicate expectations clearly and provide families with appropriate ways to stay informed about school life.

**CTA:** Ask about the current school programme

**Approval gates:** Confirm current activities, academic-year dates, photo/video permissions, student consent, safeguarding, event details and any partner names or logos. Do not reuse old gallery images without permission review.

## 3. Professional English

**SEO title:** Professional English Language Learning | AGI  
**Meta description:** Ask about English learning options designed around workplace communication and professional goals.

# English for professional communication
Clear communication in English can support collaboration, customer interaction, presentations, correspondence and participation in international work environments.

A professional English programme should be shaped around the learner's role, current level and practical communication needs. Before describing a course as active, AGI should confirm its curriculum, trainer, format, duration, assessment, schedule and fees.

### Possible learning objectives
- writing clear professional emails;
- participating in meetings and discussions;
- presenting information and explaining ideas;
- handling workplace conversations and customer interactions;
- developing vocabulary relevant to a professional field.

**CTA:** Discuss your professional English needs

**Approval gates:** Do not imply that this course is an Anglia examination or qualification unless it is formally part of that pathway. Confirm current availability, instructors, course design and fees.

## 4. English for healthcare and medical contexts

**SEO title:** English for Healthcare Communication | AGI  
**Meta description:** Enquire about English language learning for healthcare and medical communication contexts.

# English for healthcare communication
Healthcare professionals and learners may need English to read professional material, communicate with colleagues, explain information and participate in international learning environments.

Any healthcare-focused English programme should be described as language education, not clinical training or a professional medical qualification. Its content should be reviewed by a qualified subject-matter expert and adapted to the learners' roles and language levels.

### Possible areas of language practice
- professional introductions and workplace communication;
- understanding general healthcare vocabulary;
- reading and summarising non-confidential professional texts;
- explaining processes in clear, respectful language;
- communicating across professional teams.

This page is a proposed content direction only. It must not imply that AGI currently offers a medical English course until the programme owner confirms the curriculum, instructor qualifications, schedule and enrolment process.

**CTA:** Enquire about healthcare English learning

**Approval gates:** Confirm the course exists, specialist reviewer, instructor credentials, target audience, syllabus, terminology review and non-clinical scope.

## 5. Examination proctoring

**SEO title:** Examination Proctoring and Invigilation | AGI  
**Meta description:** Ask AGI about examination supervision arrangements for approved examinations and institutions.

# Examination supervision and proctoring
Examination proctoring involves supervising candidates during an assessment according to the rules set by the examination provider or institution.

Where AGI is authorised and equipped to provide this service, the page should explain the supported examinations, venue or delivery format, candidate identification requirements, equipment, booking process and applicable fees.

### Before requesting a session
The examination provider must confirm that the venue or proctor is acceptable for the specific examination. Requirements may differ between providers and tests.

AGI should not imply that it can administer, certify or validate an examination on behalf of a provider without written authorisation.

**CTA:** Ask about proctoring availability

**Approval gates:** Confirm active service, written provider approvals, supported exams, facilities, identity checks, privacy/data retention, incident procedure, fees and session capacity.

## 6. Teacher development and professional learning

**SEO title:** Teacher Development and Professional Learning | AGI  
**Meta description:** Explore teacher-development activities and ask about current professional learning opportunities at AGI.

# Supporting educators in their professional learning
Professional learning can help educators reflect on their practice, develop subject knowledge and explore approaches to teaching and assessment.

AGI's teacher-development content should distinguish between:
- workshops or professional-learning events organised by AGI;
- independent courses delivered by an external provider;
- examination pathways such as Anglia C.I.T.E.;
- formal qualifications awarded by a named awarding organisation.

These are not interchangeable. Every page must state who delivers the programme, who assesses participants, who awards any certificate and what the participant actually receives.

### Current opportunities
List only workshops, courses or programmes that have a confirmed owner, schedule, format, fee and enrolment route.

**CTA:** Ask about teacher-development opportunities

**Approval gates:** Confirm providers, trainers, credentials, course content, award/credit status, dates, fees and any legal or immigration implications. Do not advertise teacher sponsorship, jobs or visas without specialist legal verification.

## 7. Educational resources

**SEO title:** Educational Resources for Learners, Families and Educators | AGI  
**Meta description:** Find practical learning resources, school guidance and educational materials from AGI.

# Useful resources for learning
Good educational resources help learners practise, families understand school expectations and educators support learning more effectively.

The resource library should be organised by audience and topic:
- **For learners:** practice activities, reading guidance and study skills.
- **For families:** school routines, attendance, homework and communication guidance.
- **For educators:** teaching resources, professional learning and classroom guidance.
- **For examination candidates:** official sample materials and preparation resources, subject to provider permissions.

Each resource should display its intended audience, subject, language, publication or review date, author/source and any usage restrictions.

**CTA:** Explore learning resources

**Approval gates:** Check authorship, accuracy, accessibility, copyright, third-party permissions, current policy alignment and translation quality.

- Never invent current programmes, fees, schedules, staff credentials or outcomes.

- Never imply AGI awards credentials issued by external providers.
- Use student images and testimonials only with documented, specific consent.
- Date all operational information and assign a content owner.
- Keep English as the master version; localise Arabic and French after approval.
