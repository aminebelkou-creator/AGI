# Education, language and opportunity — with roots in Agadir.

American Group International (AGI) is an education organisation based in Agadir, Morocco. Its activities bring together school education, inclusive learning support, English-language learning and examinations through Anglia Morocco, and selected learning opportunities for adults, educators and professionals.

The purpose of this page is to help you understand the organisation, the people responsible for its work and the different programmes available. Each programme has its own scope, requirements and responsible provider.

**CTA:** Explore our programmes  
**Secondary CTA:** Contact AGI

### Section — One organisation, distinct learning pathways

**Heading:** Different goals. Clear routes to learning.

AGI's work serves people at different stages of their educational and professional lives.

- **School education:** information for families considering the AGI school in Agadir, including the learning approach, admissions and student support.
- **Inclusive learning:** information about educational support and family collaboration, with specialist services described separately where they are available.
- **English learning and examinations:** local information about Anglia Examinations, English classes, preparation resources and candidate support.

**Heading:** Affordable. Creative. Inclusive.

**Creative**

**Inclusive**  
Learners bring different strengths, backgrounds and support needs. We value listening to families, understanding the learner and explaining the educational support that is genuinely available.

# Start with a conversation about your child's learning.

Choosing a school is an important decision. The admissions process should give families a clear understanding of the school programme, the information needed for placement and the next steps before an application is submitted.

Contact the AGI admissions team to discuss your child's educational background, the grade you are considering and the documents that may be needed.

**Primary CTA:** Start an admissions enquiry  
**Secondary CTA:** Arrange a school visit

### Section — The admissions journey

**Heading:** Four steps to a considered decision.

**01 — Make an enquiry**  
Tell the admissions team which grade or school stage you are considering and share the best way to contact you. The team can explain current availability and the information required for an initial discussion.

**02 — Visit and ask questions**  
Arrange a visit to understand the learning environment. Ask about curriculum, language support, student life, assessment, policies and the services available to families.

**03 — Share the relevant school information**  
The admissions team will explain which records and documents are required. These may include previous school reports, identification and other information relevant to the learner's placement. The current official document list must be provided by the school.

**04 — Discuss placement and next steps**  
The school reviews the information in line with its approved admissions and placement policies. The team explains the outcome, any conditions, fees and the next steps. A grade or admission outcome should not be promised before the review is complete.

### Section — Preparing for an enquiry

**Heading:** Information that helps us understand the learner.

Before contacting the school, families may wish to prepare:
- The learner's age and current school stage.
- The name and location of the previous school, if applicable.
- Recent school reports or transcripts, if available.
- The language(s) the learner uses and their experience of English.
- Any learning information or support arrangements the family wishes to discuss confidentially.
- Questions about the curriculum, school day, fees and family communication.

**Privacy note:** Sensitive educational, disability or health information should be requested only when necessary, through an appropriate confidential process. Do not collect it in a general public enquiry form.

### Section — Placement

**Heading:** Placement should reflect the learner's educational history.

A placement discussion may consider age, previous learning, school records, language development and the school's approved policy. The admissions team should explain how placement is assessed and whether any additional information or assessment is needed.

AGI should not promise automatic grade equivalence, credit transfer or acceptance of previous coursework. These decisions depend on the applicable school policy and any relevant external requirements.

### Section — Fees and payment

**Heading:** Understand the costs before you apply.

Families should receive the current fee schedule and a clear explanation of:
- Registration or inscription fees.
- Tuition and payment frequency.
- What is included in the tuition.
- Any separate examination, resource, activity or service costs.
- Payment deadlines and accepted methods.
- Refund and cancellation terms.

**Publication gate:** Link only to the current, approved fee schedule with its effective academic year. Do not reproduce historic amounts from old pages or the launch checklist as current prices.

### Section — Frequently asked questions

**Can I visit before applying?**  
Families are encouraged to contact the school to arrange a visit and discuss the programme before making an application.

**Can my child join from another school system?**  
Contact the admissions team with the learner's school history and available records. The school will explain its placement review and any additional requirements.

**Does my child need to be fluent in English?**  
The learner's English level and available language support should be discussed with the school. Do not promise admission or a specific support arrangement before the learner's needs and current provision have been reviewed.

**Which documents are required?**  
The admissions team should provide the current document list for the learner's circumstances. Avoid relying on an old downloadable checklist.

**Where can I find the fees?**  
Request the current fee schedule from the admissions team or use the approved fees page for the current academic year.

**How long does the process take?**  
The school should provide a response-time estimate only if it has approved and can consistently meet that service standard.

Tell the admissions team what you need to know. They can explain the current process and help you decide on the next step.

Anglia Examinations offers English-language assessments for learners at different stages. The Anglia Morocco section is designed to help candidates understand the examination pathway, prepare for an appropriate level and find out about local arrangements.

**Heading:** Start at the level that reflects your ability.

Anglia's General English pathway is presented across ten levels, from First Step to Mastery (C2). The examination descriptions and assessment components should be taken from the current official Anglia specifications.

A level assessment or placement conversation can help a candidate understand where to begin. It should not be described as an official examination result unless it is one.

### Section — What is assessed?

**Heading:** English is more than one skill.

Depending on the examination and level, assessment may cover:
- Listening.
- Reading.
- Writing.
- Speaking.

Candidates should consult the specification for their exact level to understand which components apply, how the examination is delivered and how results are reported.

### Section — Business English

**Heading:** English for professional communication.

Anglia's Business English pathway is separate from the General English route. It is intended for learners who want to develop and demonstrate English in professional contexts.

The page should identify the current levels, assessment components, intended candidate and official qualification details. Do not assume that the Business English levels have the same regulatory or awarding status as the highest General English qualifications.

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** ANGLIA EXAMINATIONS

# C.I.T.E. — understand the examination and what it demonstrates.

The Certificate for International Teachers of English (C.I.T.E.) is an Anglia Examinations assessment for people who teach English or are preparing to teach it. It is designed to assess English-language competence in a teaching-related context.

### Important distinction

**C.I.T.E. is not, by itself, a professional teaching qualification or a teaching licence.**

Candidates who need a teaching qualification, professional licence or TEFL/TESOL certificate should check the requirements of the relevant employer, regulator or training provider separately.

### Before you register

Ask the Anglia Morocco team for the current:
- Examination specification and available versions.
- Recommended language level and entry guidance.
- Assessment format and duration.
- Examination dates and registration process.
- Fees and candidate support arrangements.
