## Editorial rules used in this draft

- Write in clear, confident US English, with a warm and respectful institutional voice.
- Speak to the reader's needs before describing the organization.
- Avoid inflated promises, generic education marketing language and unsupported superlatives.
- Keep the original AGI meaning and institutional terminology.
- Do not publish sample testimonials as real testimonials.
- Do not use child names or identifiable child photographs while the relevant written-consent record is not approved.
- Do not publish open claims in `v2/_build/claims.json`.
- Fees, dates and contact details must be taken from the current facts register at publication time. Prices and calendars are deliberately omitted from evergreen copy below.
- Proposed wording is not an institutional approval. Policy and legal language must retain the authoritative source wording.
