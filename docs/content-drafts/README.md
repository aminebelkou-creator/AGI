## Editorial workflow

1. Review the English meaning and confirm the page purpose and audience.
2. Resolve all linked facts and claims in `content/facts/fact-register.csv` and `content/governance/claims-register.csv`.
3. Obtain the relevant school, leadership, partner, specialist or legal approval.
4. Review Arabic and French as independent localisations; do not publish machine-only translations.
5. Confirm images, names, testimonials, third-party text and other media rights.
6. Confirm every form, phone number, email address and call to action is operational.
7. Record owner, approval, effective date and next review date.

## Important

- Draft wording is not approved wording.
- Do not copy historical prices, schedules, staff titles or partner claims into current pages without verification.
- Do not imply that AGI awards a qualification when another provider or awarding body is responsible.
- Do not present C.I.T.E. as a teaching licence or standalone TEFL/TESOL qualification.
- Do not promote clinical or therapeutic services without specialist, safeguarding and legal review.
- Do not publish identifiable student material without specific documented consent.

The copy packs are intended to be reviewed and improved before any content is imported into the live site or a CMS.
