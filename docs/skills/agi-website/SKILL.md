# AGI website – how to work on it

## Sources of truth (read before changing content)
- Project: `C:\Users\belko\AGI` (GitHub `aminebelkou-creator/AGI`, private). Built site: `v2/` (151 pages: 69 main, 66 journal, `fr/`, `ar/`).
- Facts (fees, dates, contacts): `v2/_build/facts.json` – the ONLY place a fee/date/contact may change.
- Claims not to publish until confirmed: `v2/_build/claims.json` (status `open`).
- Photo/name privacy holds: `v2/_build/privacy.json` (`hide_photos` minus `approved`).
- Open questions for Julie: `docs/AI-ASSISTANT-BRIEF.md` (regenerate: `python v2/_build/assistant_brief.py`).

## Non-negotiable rules
1. Never invent or "improve" facts. Only facts.json / the spec. If two sources disagree, stop and ask.
2. Keep placeholders visible AND machine-readable until Julie resolves them: `data-placeholder="photo|fact|testimonial|team:<id>"`, `data-verify="<id>"` (gold text, grey photo frames). If an editor/importer strips `data-*` attributes, report it – never silently lose them.
3. Children's privacy (Moroccan law 09-08): no child's name or photo without written parental consent. Keep "Photo hidden until parental consent is confirmed".
4. Never publish open claims: "exclusive" franchise; expansion to other cities/Mauritania/USA; "Director of Global Operations"; Anglia "dependent on the UK Ministry of Education"; British Council or QCA recognition.
5. Anglia wording exactly: "official partner in Morocco of Chichester College Group, UK"; "the four highest levels are awarded by AIM Qualifications and Assessment Group, regulated by Ofqual"; "the Mastery (C2) qualification carries UCAS tariff points".
6. Quote exactly: "Grade placement is not lowered due to missing documents, language gaps, or academic gaps."
7. One website: never link to angliamorocco.org (it redirects to the Anglia section). Mailboxes j.wilburn@ / a.lam@angliamorocco.org stay. Official school email: americanecoleagadir@gmail.com.
8. Copyright: no third-party tests/texts/images (the Pearson placement test is excluded on purpose).
9. Keep AGI's own wording in policies and the handbook; suggest edits, apply only what Julie approves.

## Design system (keep – do not redesign)
- Colors: navy #0A3161, deep #061529, red #B22234, gold #C9A227, paper #F6F5F1, grey #5B6472 / muted #646A75.
- Fonts: Newsreader (titles), Inter (text); Arabic: Noto Naskh Arabic, right-to-left, no italics.
- Anglia pages (approved by Anglia): same layout, accents #03379F / #0082D0 / #000F23, Anglia Morocco logo `assets/web/anglia-anglia-moroccool3.webp` in the hub hero, the "Why Anglia" strip and as a page badge.
- Every page ends with a call-to-action band; long AGI originals sit in a collapsible "Full text as published by AGI".
- Mobile: no horizontal overflow at 390 px; WCAG 2.1 AA; one H1 per page.

## Sitewright specifics
- Local proof of concept: http://localhost:8090 (container `agi-sitewright-poc`, volume `agi-sw-data`). MCP: `http://localhost:8090/mcp` (OAuth).
- Start every session with `get_guide` / `get_capabilities`; use `preview_page` to SEE results.
- Arabic: Sitewright sets no page-level direction. Make Arabic pages **fork** variants whose source wraps content in `<div dir="rtl" lang="ar">`; check header/footer direction with `preview_page`.
- Facts → a dataset (e.g. `facts`) referenced by pages, so a fee changes in one place.
- Forms: `{{sw-form}}` definitions; submissions contain families' data – their storage location must be decided (law 09-08) before launch.
- Redirects are NOT managed in Sitewright: they live at the host (EdgeOne `middleware.js`, generated by `v2/_build/prepare_edgeone.py`).
- Always `list_revisions` before large edits; `restore_revision` if something goes wrong.

## Fidelity – nothing may be lost
- After any import or bulk edit: Sitewright `fidelity_check` / `compare_to_source`, then publish to a folder and run
  `python v2/_build/compare_published.py <published-folder> index.html fees.html ar/index.html` (text ≥95%, all facts, links, markers, lang/dir).
- Full checks on the static build: `python v2/_build/check_all.py`.

## Deploy (EdgeOne Makers; proof-of-concept account hotelhersedorparis@gmail.com, international site)
- Build copy: `python v2/_build/prepare_edgeone.py` (preview/noindex; `--production` at launch). Never upload `.env*`.
- Deploy: `edgeone makers deploy "%USERPROFILE%\agi-edgeone-deploy" -n agi-website-poc -a overseas` – ALWAYS `-a overseas` (Global excluding Mainland China; no ICP filing). Max 25 MB per file.
- Verify: `python v2/_build/test_deploy.py https://agi-website-poc.edgeone.dev` (must be all PASS).
