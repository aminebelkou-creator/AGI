## 4. A more natural alternative: interview, then edit

People often sound more convincing answering questions than reading lines. Film Julie answering these, then edit her answers into the structure of section 1.

1. Take us back to 2013. What did that first room look like? Who were the five children?
2. Why did you start a school at all?
3. What did you see happening to families in Agadir that you wanted to change?
4. What does "affordable, creative and inclusive" mean to you, day to day?
5. Tell me about a child who arrived with very little English. What happened?
6. Why does AGI never move a child down a grade because of a language gap?
7. What do you want a parent to feel when they leave your school after a visit?
8. Why does a child's feeling of safety matter so much to you?
9. What makes you proudest after all these years?
10. If you could say one sentence to a parent who is hesitating, what would it be?

Tip: ask Julie to repeat the question in her answer ("When I started the school in 2013…"), so each answer stands on its own in the edit.

## 6. Production checklist

- [ ] **Consent:** written consent from every parent for every child who appears; consent from teachers; no child's full name on screen.
- [ ] **Facts:** only the verified facts in this script. Nothing about expansion, other cities, Mauritania or the USA until confirmed.
- [ ] **Equipment:** recent phone filming 4K at 25 fps · tripod · clip-on (lavalier) microphone for Julie · reflector or window light.
- [ ] **Sound:** quiet room for the interview; record 30 seconds of room silence for the edit.
- [ ] **Location:** tidy classroom; AGI seal or flags visible but not staged.
- [ ] **Music:** soft, warm, royalty-free (licensed); low under Julie's voice.
- [ ] **Brand:** AGI navy (#0A3161), gold (#C9A227), red (#B22234); fonts Newsreader (titles) and Inter (subtitles).
- [ ] **Subtitles:** burned-in for social media; separate subtitle files (.srt) in English, French and Arabic for the website and YouTube.
- [ ] **Exports:** 16:9 (website, YouTube), 9:16 (reels, stories, WhatsApp status), 1:1 (feed).
- [ ] **Placement:** top of the "Our story" page and the Parents page on the website; pinned post on Facebook and Instagram; WhatsApp channel.
- [ ] **Approval:** Julie approves the final cut before publishing.
