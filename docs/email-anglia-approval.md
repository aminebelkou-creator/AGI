Please reply with corrections where needed, or "approved" if the wording is acceptable.

## Facts to confirm
1. Anglia Examinations has been based in Chichester, England, since 1993.
2. AGI has used Anglia in its language programs since 2013 and became the Anglia franchise holder for Morocco in 2021.
3. Anglia offers ten levels, from First Step to Masters (C2), mapped to the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR).
4. There is no minimum age; young learners usually start at about four.
5. Every level assesses listening, reading, writing and speaking.
6. Anglia International Business English is offered at four levels, from A2+ to C1.
