# AGI website – proposed growth plan

*Proposal · Version 1, October 2026 · Owner: Amine, with Julie A. Wilburn*
*Builds on the current website (`v2/`), the launch checklist (`v2/LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`) and the marketing strategy (`docs/marketing-strategy.md`).*

This plan lists what to **add** to the website after the current build. It excludes what is already in progress before launch: real photos, real testimonials, and the facts Julie must confirm.

**Effort:** S = a few hours · M = 1–3 days · L = a week or more.
**Depends on:** what must exist first. **Can start now:** no input needed from AGI.

---

## Phase 1 – Before launch

Gaps parents would notice on day one.

| # | Addition | Why it matters | Effort | Depends on | Can start now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **French version, then Arabic** – start with Home, Parents, Our story, Admissions, Contact, Forms; then Arabic (right-to-left) | Most Moroccan parents read French more comfortably than English; Arabic reaches more families. The first rebuild (`app/`) already has French/Arabic work to reuse | L | Native-speaker review of translations | Yes (drafts) |
| 2 | **Forms that really deliver** – connect the `app/` backend: all 10 form types + contact, saved in a database, email alert to the office | Today forms open the parent's email app; on phones without one, requests are silently lost | M | Hosting for the backend | Yes |
| 3 | **"Page not found" (404) page** – friendly message, links to Admissions, Programs, Contact, Forms | Old links from WordPress, Facebook and Google will land here | S | – | Yes |
| 4 | **Accessibility statement and safeguarding page** – how AGI protects children, child-protection contact, photo policy | Parents and partners look for it; it supports trust and the photo/testimonial consents | S | Julie confirms safeguarding contact and policy | Yes (draft) |
| 5 | **Privacy-friendly visit statistics** (e.g. Plausible or Umami, no cookies) | Without it we can't tell whether the popup, the story page or ads bring visits and applications | S | Choice of tool (free self-hosted or paid) | Yes |

**Recommended order:** 3 → 2 → 1 → 5 (4 in parallel once Julie answers).

## Phase 2 – First months after launch

Trust and conversions, timed with the marketing calendar before registration days (August 28 & 31).

| # | Addition | Why it matters | Effort | Depends on | Can start now |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Downloadable prospectus (PDF)** in English and French, generated from the site content | Parents share it on WhatsApp with their partner or grandparents | M | Photos for the best result | Yes (text version) |
| 7 | **Visit booking with real time slots** – parents choose an available slot instead of a "preferred date" | Removes back-and-forth by phone; more visits actually booked | M | Office agrees available slots; forms backend (#2) | After #2 |
| 8 | **Events & open mornings page** – "Coffee with the founder", registration days, Anglia exam sessions, with "Add to calendar" and WhatsApp reminders | Supports the open-morning strategy and the run-up to registration | S–M | Dates from AGI | Yes (structure) |
| 9 | **Google reviews on the site** – real ratings from AGI's Google Business Profile, plus an invitation to happy parents to leave one | Independent reviews are among the strongest trust signals | S | Google Business Profile access | Partly |
| 10 | **"Why AGI" comparison page** – AGI vs typical international schools on fees, class size, language support, accreditation | Answers the question parents are really asking | S | Verified facts only | Yes |
| 11 | **Fees calculator** – choose grade(s) and number of children → monthly cost, inscription, yearly total | Makes "affordable" concrete | S | Julie confirms sibling discounts / payment plans, if any | After answer |

## Summary roadmap

| When | Website additions | Marketing alignment (`marketing-strategy.md`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Now → launch** | 404 page · forms backend · French (then Arabic) · statistics · safeguarding/accessibility | Facts confirmed, photos and consents collected |
| **Launch → 3 months** | Prospectus · events & open mornings · Google reviews · "Why AGI" page | Founder film, "Our story", parent stories, first open mornings |

| **After registration** | Alumni stories · careers · virtual tour | Back-to-school story, results review |
| **Next year** | Parent area · AGI Connect assistant | Retention and referrals |

| Measure | Tool |
|---|---|
| Visits to Parents / Our story pages and popup clicks | Statistics (#5) |
| Visits booked, forms sent, by form type | Forms backend (#2) |
| Open-morning attendance | Events page (#8) |
| Google rating and number of reviews | Google Business Profile (#9) |
| Applications → enrollments, by source ("How did you hear about us?") | Forms backend + office |
