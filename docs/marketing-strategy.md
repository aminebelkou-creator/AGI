## 1. The idea in one sentence

**One room, five children, 2013 – and a belief that an American education shouldn't be only for the wealthy.**

Parents don't choose a curriculum; they choose someone they trust with their child. Julie's story is the most credible proof of what AGI promises: an education that is **affordable, creative and inclusive**.

### The story arc (for every format)
1. **The beginning** – 2013, one room in Agadir, five children.
2. **The problem** – good families shut out by fees, or held back by systems that punish a child for still learning a language.
3. **The choice** – build something different: affordable, creative, inclusive.
4. **The proof** – 46+ nationalities, a K–12 school in Hay Nahda, Anglia Morocco across the twelve regions.
5. **The promise today** – your child will be known, supported and never held back for a language gap.
6. **The invitation** – come and see the school.

## 4. Channels and options

Ranked from most to least impact for a school in Agadir.

### 4.1 Founder film (60–90 seconds) – the anchor
- Julie at the school telling the story arc above in her own words: the first room, why she started, one child she remembers, her promise to parents.
- Filmed on a good phone; natural light; classroom sounds; subtitles in **English, French and Arabic**.
- Cut-downs: 15 s and 30 s for social and ads; one vertical version (9:16).
- Feeds the website, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, open mornings.

### 4.2 "Our story" page on the website
- Scroll-through timeline: 2013 one room → Hay Nahda → 46+ nationalities → Anglia franchise 2021 → today.
- Founder film at the top, a photo at each step, the founder's letter, ends with **Book a visit**.
- Can be built now with placeholders, like the rest of the site.

### 4.3 Real parent stories – the proof
Three short videos or written stories, "before and after":
1. **The fresh start** – a child who arrived with little or no English.
2. **The safe harbor** – a child who needed individual support.
3. **The bridge to the world** – a graduate now studying abroad.

Real families only, with **written consent** (and photo consent for children). These convert better than anything AGI says about itself. *The sample testimonials on the site must be replaced by these.*

### 4.4 Social media series (Facebook, Instagram)
- **"From 5 to 46+"** – weekly: one student, one nationality, one moment.
- **"Ask Julie"** – monthly short video answering real parent questions (fees, English, special needs, homework).
- **Behind the scenes** – Saturday Anglia classes, park outings, spelling bee, graduation.
- Every post ends with one action: Book a visit, or WhatsApp us.

### 4.5 "Coffee with the founder" open mornings
- Monthly, small groups: meet Julie, tour the school, sit in on a class.
- More in line with the trust message than ads; each one creates content (photos, questions for "Ask Julie").
- Extra sessions before registration (spring, then June–August).

### 4.6 WhatsApp
- Broadcast channel for interested families: open mornings, registration dates, Anglia exam sessions.
- Entry point: the WhatsApp button on every page of the site (+212 688 739 393).
- Quick, personal replies – for Moroccan families this is often where the decision is made.

### 4.7 Local press and partners
- Pitch: *"An American woman who started a school in one room in Agadir."*
- Targets: Moroccan media, regional radio, English-language outlets in Morocco, Anglia and Chichester College Group partner news.

### 4.8 Paid ads – only once 4.1 and 4.3 exist
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads to parents in Agadir and the Souss: founder film → `parents.html`.
- Best period: May–August, before registration days.
- Measure cost per visit booked, not likes.

## 9. Rules

- **Only verified facts.** Anything not yet confirmed stays out of films, ads and press. A published video is hard to correct.
- **Real people, real consent.** No invented testimonials; written consent for every story and every child's photo.
- **Three languages.** English, French and Arabic – French and Arabic carry the story furthest with Moroccan parents.
- **One action per message.** Book a visit, or WhatsApp us.
- **Julie's voice.** Warm, direct, personal – the way she would speak to a parent at the school gate.
