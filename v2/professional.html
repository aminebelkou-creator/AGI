Home / Programs / Teacher training & professional

For teachers, clinicians and professionals.

AGI's work goes beyond the school day: certification for teachers, English for professional practice, exam invigilation for distance learners, and coaching.

ApplyBook a visit
C.I.T.E. / TESOLFor English teachers
Anglia's Certificate for International Teachers of English: no entry qualifications required, mapped to the CEFR, and a lifelong recognition of teaching competence. AGI also partners with Bridge Education Group for accredited TEFL/TESOL courses.
Teacher training programFor classroom practice
English teachers test and train in AGI classrooms to strengthen their language and their teaching.
USA teacher sponsorshipFor aspiring educators
Certification sponsorship for private-school teaching in the United States. Requires a bachelor's degree, a clear background check and a sponsoring school. Fee: $60. Not valid for U.S. public schools.
Independent course studiesSelf-paced
Graduate-level, self-taught modules using books and journals; certificates approved by the American Canadian Board.
Proctoring servicesExam invigilation
Paper and online exams invigilated at AGI for distance learners of North American and British universities, companies and certification bodies. Monday to Thursday, 9:00–17:00. A 200 MAD (15 EUR) administration fee plus 200 MAD (15 EUR) per exam hour.
Corporate & business EnglishFor organisations
Anglia Business English in four steps from A2+ to C1, on site at your company or at AGI.
Medical EnglishFor clinicians
English for medical professionals: patient communication, handovers, seminars and medical papers.
ADHD holistic coachingIn Agadir and online
One-to-one coaching for children and adults on focus, organisation and time management, with Julie Wilburn, an accredited Holistic Therapeutic Coach (IPHM, ICAHP).
