Global Network · Franchise & Partnerships

Bring the AGI standard to your region.

AGI holds the Anglia Examinations franchise for Morocco and operates from Agadir across the Kingdom's twelve regions. We work with schools, language centres, freelance teachers and international institutions — and franchise opportunities are open in the English market for the right candidates.

Two ways to build with AGI

Model I

Franchise

An Anglia Morocco centre under your ownership, anywhere in the Kingdom's twelve regions. You get the examinations licence, the placement and testing system, training, and the AGI standard behind it. Enquiries run through the Agadir headquarters.

— Anglia examinations licence & materials — Teacher training and C.I.T.E. certification — Placement testing & level mapping to the CEFR — Territory agreement within the 12 regions

Model II

Institutional Partnership

For existing schools, language institutes and colleges: Anglia examination provision inside your campus, university pathway agreements, and co-designed English or academic programmes under a shared quality framework.

— Co-designed English & academic programmes — University pathway agreements (UK, USA, India) — Anglia testing centre inside your campus — Shared quality framework & annual review

Who we work with

Examination boards, colleges, placement partners and professional bodies.

United Kingdom

Chichester College Group International

The international arm of a seven-college group in the south of England — Chichester, Crawley, Worthing, Brinsbury, Brighton MET, Northbrook and Haywards Heath. Signed 2025.

Teacher training

Bridge Education Group

A global leader in teacher training and TEFL/TESOL certification. Partnership announced November 2025, opening professional development to educators across Morocco.

University placement

Leverage Edu

Official regional representation for Souss and southern Morocco since March 2026 — private and public university placement with housing assistance.

Plantation, Florida

American High School

Cognia-accredited online diploma programme and a Yass Prize awardee. Partnership announced 2025; the diploma is accepted in the U.S. and internationally.

United Kingdom

RB College

UCAS-registered UK institution offering academic and vocational programmes, including work-based Master of Business Administration routes.

Chichester, since 1993

Anglia Examinations England

AGI has been the Anglia franchise holder for Morocco since 2021 and has used the Anglia system in its language programmes since 2013.

For teacherpreneurs

AGI Learning Pods

Small-group programmes run by freelance teachers and parent groups under the Anglia system — inclusive, creative and affordable.

Membership

American School Counselor Association
