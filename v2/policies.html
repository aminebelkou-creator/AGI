Policies for families.

The rules we hold ourselves and our families to, in brief. The full texts are part of the AGI Academic Handbook and are available from the office.

Request the full textsReport an absence
Attendance
K–12 students must attend 90% of the school year to be promoted. Parents give a dated, signed excuse for any absence within two days of the student's return. Arriving after 9:00 is a late arrival; after 10:00 it counts as a full-day absence. Five unexcused late arrivals equal one unexcused absence.
Promotion & retention
Promotion follows the grade-level criteria: at least 64% (D) in each core subject – mathematics, science, language arts and social studies – 90% attendance, maturity shown through the year and the English assessment. Students who are not promoted may attend summer remediation and be retested.
Provisional placement
Every new student sits English/ESL and academic assessments before admission, whatever system they come from, so placement holds even when previous records are incomplete.
Parent responsibility
Enrollment begins with a 90-day adjustment period and shared expectations on attendance, participation, safeguarding and following up on learning at home.
Refunds & complaints
One procedure across all divisions – the school, AGI Learning Pods, iHeart therapeutic services and Anglia Morocco – covering enrollment, refunds, complaints and counseling. The annual inscription is non-refundable.
School counseling
Guidance from the school counselor is advisory: it supports academic success and personal growth but is not medical diagnosis or therapy.
Media
How photos, video and live streams of school events may be used on the website, social media and print – always with consent and safeguarding first.
Service hours
High school graduation requires 40 documented volunteer hours, ten each year, approved by the school.
Homework
Nightly homework grows from 10–20 minutes in kindergarten to 2–3 hours in the last years of high school.
