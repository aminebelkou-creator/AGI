Home / Programs / Adult high school diploma

Finish high school, on your schedule.

For learners aged 16 and over who left school before graduating: a low-cost, self-directed American high school completion program, studied by correspondence or online.

ApplyBook a visit

How it works

Offered in partnership with American Worldwide Academy. AGI's first adult graduate received their diploma in 2019.

Study at your own pace
Work through each course independently, online or by correspondence, with tutorial help when you need it.
Tests in Agadir
Every test is proctored on site at the AGI centre. Graduation requires a passing score of 80 percent.
English included
All students learn English and sit an English exam as part of the program.
Support after graduation
Enrolment and graduation verification, letters of recommendation and official transcripts for further study.
