Anglia FAQ

Answers about Anglia exams – levels, ages, durations and certificates.

Book a free level testExam dates
What are the Anglia exams?

Official English certificates from Anglia Examinations (Chichester, England, since 1993; part of Chichester College Group). They evaluate reading, listening, speaking and writing, from initiation to expertise, helping students reach the level they want step by step.

Which levels do they certify?

10 levels in three groups – young learners (First Step, Junior, Primary, Preliminary), intermediate (Elementary, Pre-Intermediate, Intermediate) and higher (Advanced, Proficiency, Masters) – plus Business English levels with business vocabulary.

How long do the exams last?

Reading and writing: 1 hour minimum and 3 hours maximum depending on the level. Listening: 10 to 30 minutes. Speaking: about 5 to 20 minutes.

Who can take them?

Anyone: students of all ages from 4 years onwards, with no upper age limit. You can start at the level you or your teacher consider appropriate.

Do I need extra classes?

No – preparation is built into the classroom curriculum. Classes at AGI are available if you want them.

Does the certificate expire?

It lasts for life, but an institution may ask for a recent one if yours is very old.

How do I know which level to take?

Take the free placement assessment; Anglia also offers an additional exam that determines your level.

Are Anglia certificates recognized by universities?

The Mastery (C2) qualification carries UCAS tariff points for UK universities. Further recognition statements to be confirmed with Anglia.

Are they affordable?

Anglia exams are priced affordably compared with other international certificates.
