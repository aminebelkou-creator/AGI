Anglia Examinations England

C.I.T.E. – Certificate for International Teachers of English

A TESOL qualification that proves both your English and your ability to teach it.

Register on angliamorocco.org
Lifelong
A qualification with no expiration date, recognized worldwide.
Open to all
No entry qualifications are required. It suits current teachers who want to validate their skills and aspiring teachers starting out.
Mapped to the CEFR
Your certificate shows your English level on the same European framework schools and employers use.
Specializations
General English, Young Learners and Very Young Learners

The 30-day teacher internship

Learn by doing, in real AGI classrooms in Agadir.

01

Week 1

Orientation to the school and its methods, and classroom observation.

02

Week 2

Co-teaching with a mentor teacher and lesson planning.

03

Week 3

Leading lessons with feedback, and assessment practice.

04

Week 4

Professional development workshops, a final survey and wrap-up.

More for educators

USA teacher sponsorship
Certification sponsorship for teaching in U.S. private schools. Requires a bachelor's degree, a clear background check and a sponsoring school. Fee: US$60. Not valid for U.S. public schools.
Mathematics in English
For classroom teachers adding mathematics to their skills, and new teacher candidates who are not math majors: teach mathematics in English (SAT, IELTS) and qualify as a mathematics specialist or coach. Courses
Teacher training program
English teachers test and train in AGI classrooms to strengthen their language and their teaching.
TEFL / TESOL with Bridge
Accredited TEFL/TESOL courses through AGI's partnership with Bridge Education Group.
Independent course studies
Graduate-level, self-paced modules using books and journals; certificates approved by the American Canadian Board.
Teach a learning pod
Freelance teachers can lead Anglia learning pods: small groups of about ten students over ten weeks, with materials supplied.
