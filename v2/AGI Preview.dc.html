One annual inscription, then ten monthly payments. Books, mathematics examinations, Anglia English examinations, dual enrolment in the USA and Morocco, and student insurance are included.

Annual inscription — paid once at enrolment

Inscription is annual and non-refundable. Enrolling students must provide prior school paperwork and a behaviour report; IEP or special-needs provision requires documentation from a licensed practitioner. English, measured every year.

AGI benchmarks every grade against the Common European Framework and assesses it with Anglia Examinations of England — ten levels, all four skills, no minimum age. Graduation target is C1.

{{ c.grade }} {{ c.age }} {{ c.level }} {{ c.skill }}

Students who do not speak English at home typically sit half a level to a full level lower in the early grades and catch up by middle school where immersion is consistent. Learners below benchmark receive accommodations or additional lessons to reach university readiness.

From the Director

“AGI is a child-centered school where students take responsibility for their learning — we prepare them to be independent, critical thinkers and caring, contributing members of society.”

Our mission is to serve the Agadir region of Morocco by providing children with a strong educational foundation following American philosophy and practice. We hold high the value of respect — of oneself, of others, and of the environment — and we believe the family is an integral component of the program.
