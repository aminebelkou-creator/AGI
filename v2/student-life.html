The school year

Highlights from the AGI calendar.

Moroccan & American holidays
The calendar marks both: Green March and Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Amazigh New Year and the Eids.
Global Play Day
A day each February given over to unstructured play, joined by schools around the world.
Anglia exams
Every spring, students certify their English level with Anglia Examinations of England.
Spelling bee
AGI students compete in the American National Spelling Bee of Morocco, held at the American Legation in Tangier. Read the story
Graduation
The year ends in June with graduation for the senior class. Journal

Service to the community

A graduation requirement for every high school student.

High school students complete 40 volunteer hours – ten each year – documented and approved by the school. Activities must benefit the community, be unpaid, and not be part of a regular job or family obligation.

Examples include community clean-ups, helping at local libraries and non-profits, tutoring younger students and helping at school events outside school hours.
