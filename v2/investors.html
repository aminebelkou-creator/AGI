Grow with AGI.

Since 2013, AGI has grown from a single room in Agadir into a K–12 school, an English examinations network across Morocco's twelve regions, and programs for teachers and professionals. We welcome conversations with partners who share our mission.

One mission, five activities

Affordable, creative and inclusive education, delivered through complementary programs.

Since 2013

K–12 micro-school

An American-curriculum school in Agadir: eight multi-grade classrooms and students of more than 46 nationalities.

Since 2021

Anglia Morocco

Anglia Examinations franchise holder for Morocco, with a network of centers and representatives across the twelve regions.

C.I.T.E. (TESOL), teacher training, a 30-day internship and U.S. private-school sponsorship.

Why the model works

Built for working families
An international standard at an accessible price reaches families that traditional international schools do not. AGI has placed hundreds of students into the American educational system with low-cost programs.
Recognized standards
Accredited by the American Council of Training and Development, recognized by four U.S. state departments of education, and official partner in Morocco of Chichester College Group, UK. Accreditation
A format that travels
Micro-schools and learning pods – small groups of about ten students – can open where demand is, without large campuses.
