Forms & requests

Apply for a place, report an absence, request a document or tell the office about a payment. Every form explains what to have ready and what happens after you send it.

How it works

Choose the request from the list. Each form takes two to five minutes and goes straight to the AGI office in Agadir.

  1. Choose a form

    Pick the request that matches what you need. If nothing fits, use the general enquiry.

  2. Fill it in

    Every field is required unless it says optional. Your browser can fill in your name and contact details for you.

  3. The office replies

    Requests are answered within one working day, Monday to Friday. Urgent? Call or send a WhatsApp message.
