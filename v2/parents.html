Questions parents ask

My child doesn't speak English yet. Can they join?

Yes. Many of our students start with little English. They join their age group, receive ESL support, and are benchmarked with the Anglia levels as they progress.

Can my child join in the middle of the year?

Yes. We welcome students at any stage. Your child sits the placement assessment and starts with the right support.

My child has special needs. How do you support them?

We build an Individualized Education Plan with you. To put the right support in place we need documentation from a licensed practitioner, along with previous school records.

What does it cost?

Tuition is 2,600 MAD a month over ten months, plus an annual inscription paid at enrollment. Books, mathematics and Anglia exams are included. See tuition & fees.

Is there a discount for brothers and sisters?

Answer to confirm with the office.

What are the school hours?

School day start and end times to confirm. Arrival after 9:00 counts as late, and after 10:00 as a full-day absence.

Do you offer transport or lunch?

Answer to confirm with the office.

Is there a uniform?

Answer to confirm with the office.

What do we need to bring?

Previous school records and a behavior report, proof of age and residency, and immunization and physical examination records.
