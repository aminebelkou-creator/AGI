Questions & answers.

The questions families ask most, with answers taken from AGI's own policies and programs.

Ask another questionWhatsApp us

Admissions

Who can enroll at AGI?

Children aged 5 by September 1 (kindergarten) up to students under 21 on the first day of school. AGI admits students of any race, religion, color, national or ethnic origin. More

What documents do we need?

Proof of age (birth certificate, passport or similar), proof of residency less than three months old, immunization and physical examination records, two years of previous school records with a behavior report, three recent photos, and transfer papers translated into English. More

How does the admissions process work?

1) Application with the non-refundable enrollment deposit, 2) an interview with parent and child, 3) an offer of admission and the tuition agreement. More

Is there an entrance test?

Yes – a free placement assessment in English and academic subjects. Grade placement is not lowered due to missing documents, language gaps, or academic gaps. More

Can my child join in the middle of the year?

Yes. Students are welcome at any stage; they sit the placement assessment and start with the right support. More

My child doesn't speak English yet. Can they join?

Yes. Students join their age group with English as a second language support and are benchmarked on the CEFR with Anglia exams. More

Do you support children with special needs?

Yes: Individualized Education Plans, small-group classes and therapy and behavioral support. Documentation from a licensed practitioner is required – a parent's description alone is not enough. More

Fees

How much is tuition?

2,600 MAD a month, paid over ten months (26,000 MAD a year). More

How much is the annual inscription?

Kindergarten–grade 1: 6,500 MAD · grades 2–6: 7,500 MAD · grades 7–8: 7,800 MAD · grades 9–11: 10,000 MAD · grade 12: 11,000 MAD. Paid once a year, non-refundable. More

What is included?

Books and learning materials, mathematics examinations, Anglia English examinations, enrollment in the USA and Morocco, and student insurance. More

Can fees be negotiated?

No. Fees are already set as low as possible to keep AGI inclusive, and discounts are already applied for all families. More

Is there a summer school?

Yes, for high school students who need to catch up or enroll late: 3,000 MAD for a one-month program. More

Is there a discount for brothers and sisters?

To be confirmed by the office. More

School life

When does the school year start and end?

2026–27: registration days August 28 and 31, first day September 7, last day and graduation June 17, 2027. More

What are the school hours?

To be confirmed by the office. Arrival after 9:00 counts as late, and after 10:00 as an absence. More

Is there a uniform?

No uniform is mentioned; a dress code applies (see the handbook). More

What happens if my child is absent?

Give the office a dated, signed excuse within two days. Students need 90% attendance to be promoted; unexcused absences lead to escalating consequences. More

How is promotion decided?

At least 64% (D) in each core subject, 90% attendance, maturity and the English assessment; summer remediation is possible. More

How much homework is there?

From 10–20 minutes a night in kindergarten to 2–3 hours in the last years of high school. More

What do high school students need to graduate?

Among other requirements, 40 volunteer hours – ten each year. More

What should my child bring?

See the school supplies list for elementary and high school. More

Anglia exams & English

What are Anglia exams?

English exams from Anglia Examinations, based in Chichester, England, since 1993 and part of Chichester College Group. They test reading, writing, listening and speaking at ten levels, from First Step to Masters (C2). More

From what age can my child take them?

From about four years old; there is no upper age limit. More

How long are the exams?

Reading and writing 1–3 hours, listening 10–30 minutes, speaking about 5–20 minutes, depending on the level. More

Does the certificate expire?

It is valid for life, though an institution may ask for a recent one. More

When are the exam sessions?

There are sessions in most months – see the 2026 exam sessions on the calendar. Other dates are possible on request. More

Can candidates with dyslexia or a disability get support?

Yes – for example extra time, supervised breaks, Braille or large print, wheelchair access or a scribe. More

Are Saturday classes available?

Yes: ages 5–18, Saturdays 2–4 pm, October to June, 500 MAD a month including the yearly Anglia exam, with BAC, TOEFL, IELTS, SAT and Mathematics-in-English preparation. More

Teachers & adults

What is C.I.T.E.?

Anglia's Certificate for International Teachers of English: a TESOL qualification mapped to the CEFR, with no entry qualifications and lifelong validity. More

Can I teach in the United States?

AGI offers certification sponsorship for U.S. private schools: bachelor's degree, clear background check and a sponsoring school; fee US$60. Not valid for public schools. More

Can adults finish high school?

Yes, from age 16 with American Worldwide Academy; every test is proctored in Agadir and graduation requires 80%. More

Do you proctor exams for other institutions?

Yes, Monday to Thursday 9:00–17:00: a 200 MAD (15 EUR) administration fee plus 200 MAD (15 EUR) per exam hour. More
