Established 2013 · Agadir, Morocco

An American education, close to home.

American Group International is the first American school in Agadir – a K–12 micro-school with programs for adults, English learners and educators, and the headquarters of Anglia Examinations in Morocco.

Six programs, one American standard.

From a child's first day of kindergarten to an adult's diploma or teaching certificate.

Ages 5–18

K–12 school

An American-curriculum micro-school from kindergarten to grade 12, with English support through the Anglia system for children who don't speak English at home.

Learn moreInclusive

Special education (SEN)

Individualized Education Plans, small-group classes and support from occupational therapy to speech-language and behavioral care.

Learn moreAge 16+

Adult high school diploma

A self-paced U.S. high school completion program with American Worldwide Academy, with every test proctored on site in Agadir.

English & Anglia exams

AGI is the Anglia Examinations headquarters for Morocco: ten CEFR-mapped levels, Saturday classes for children and courses for adults.

Teacher training & professional

The C.I.T.E. teaching certificate, exam proctoring, business and medical English, and ADHD holistic coaching.

Why families choose AGI

A small school where every learner is known by name.

Inclusive by design

Mainstream and special-needs learners study side by side, with individual plans where they need them.

Creative curriculum

American pedagogy taught with imagination, drawing on Montessori and Waldorf ideas.

Genuinely affordable

An international standard within reach of Moroccan families, with books and exams included.

English for life

Every grade is benchmarked on the CEFR and assessed with Anglia Examinations of England.

From the director

“AGI is a child-centered school where students take responsibility for their learning.”

We prepare children to be independent, critical thinkers and caring, contributing members of society. We hold high the value of respect – of oneself, of others and of the environment – and we believe the family is an integral part of the program.

Joining AGI

Students are welcome at any stage. Every new student sits a free placement test.

01

Enquire & visit

Tours are arranged individually, so you can meet the team and see the classrooms.

02

Placement test

A free assessment, including the Anglia level test for language learners.

03

Documents

Previous school records, a behavior report, and proof of age, residency and immunization.

04

Enroll

Pay the annual inscription and confirm the monthly plan on registration days.
