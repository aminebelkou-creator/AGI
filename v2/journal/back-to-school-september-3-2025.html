Back to School September 8th, 2025

AdmissionsJune 24, 2025

First Day of classes officially starts on September 8th, 2025

We are asking all families to see us starting mid-August 2025 for enrolments. All students with outstanding balances and all new families must complete all paperwork and financial obligations before children can enter the classroom. So, please make plans early. All students entering grade 9 to the 12th grade must have a meeting with the director to pick the high school program that is needed. Counsel and guidance can't take place if students do not have a plan for the future. High School is a serious time, and it should be something all family members need to take part in.

Julie Wilburn, Director

PS Can't wait to see you in the Fall, have a great summer!
