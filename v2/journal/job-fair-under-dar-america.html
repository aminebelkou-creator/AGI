NewsJune 24, 2025

Archived post from June 24, 2025. Fees, dates and programs may have changed – see the current tuition & fees and calendar.

American Group International at the Second Edition of the English Teachers Recruitment Forum — Expanding Educational Opportunities Across Morocco

American Group International was honoured to participate in the Second Edition of the English Teachers Recruitment Forum, organized by Dar America Casablanca and held on June 22, 2025, at the Hyatt Regency Hotel.

This impactful event brought together educational institutions, international partners, and passionate educators—all committed to enhancing English language education throughout Morocco and promoting English as a medium of instruction in various academic disciplines.

For American Group International, this was more than an event—it was a powerful opportunity to present our mission, connect with fellow educational leaders, and build bridges of collaboration that will support students and teachers from Agadir and across Morocco.

We were honoured to meet:

Ms. Marissa D. Scott, Consul General of the United States

Ms. Bouchra Arrif, represented by Mr. Mohamed Dib, Director of the Regional Academy of Education and Training of Casablanca (ANFA Directorate)

Ms. E. Rose Costes, Public Affairs Officer at Dar America

Ms. Jenny McGevna McGrady, Cultural Affairs Officer at Dar America

Mr. Omar Aassou, Executive Director of Tamdolt Association — with whom we explored promising opportunities for future cooperation.

Special thanks to Mr. Adil Sarroukh for his excellent coordination of the event, and to Mr. Jawad Meniani for the kind invitation and outstanding organization of this essential forum.

We also extend our heartfelt appreciation to our valued partners at English Castle and other dedicated schools throughout the region who are helping Anglia Examinations spread and flourish across Morocco.

At American Group International, our mission goes beyond academics — we are deeply committed to serving underserved and less fortunate communities in Morocco, working to ensure that every learner, no matter their background or location, has access to quality English education and global opportunities.
