NewsApril 6, 2026

Julie Wilburn Recognized as Holistic Therapeutic Coach

We are excited to share that Julie Wilburn is now officially recognized as a Holistic Therapeutic Coach, accredited by the prestigious International Practitioners of Holistic Medicine (IPHM) and International Compliance Assurance for Holistic Practitioners (ICAHP).

Through her work with iHeart Women & Family Development Institute, a division of American Group USA and American Group International, Julie provides compassionate, individualized support and specializes in:

💜 Spectrum Disorders💜 ADHD💜 Play Therapy💜 Family Services

With a whole-person approach to wellness, growth, and development, Julie is dedicated to empowering individuals and strengthening families across communities.

This achievement reflects a continued commitment to excellence, international standards, and meaningful impact both locally and globally.
