English, measured step by step.

AGI is the headquarters of Anglia Examinations in Morocco. Children and adults learn English in small groups and certify their level with exams from England – starting with a free level test.

ApplyBook a visit

Anglia Examinations

Based in Chichester, England, since 1993. AGI has used Anglia in its language programs since 2013 and became the franchise holder for Morocco in 2021.

Ten levels
From young learner to expert user, mapped to the Common European Framework (CEFR). There is no minimum age; young learners start at about four.
All four skills
Every level tests listening, reading, writing and speaking.
Business English
Anglia International Business English, at four levels from A2+ to C1.
Recognized in the UK
Delivered with AIM Qualifications, regulated by Ofqual. The Proficiency level is listed by UCAS as evidence of English for British universities.

Classes & prices

Groups never exceed ten students. Level testing is free; class times are agreed after the test.

English benchmarks at AGI

How English develops through the K–12 school. Students who don't speak English at home often start half a level lower and catch up by middle school.

Anglia across Morocco

From its Agadir headquarters, Anglia Morocco works with schools, language centres and teachers in the Kingdom's twelve regions. Schools can become Anglia exam centres, and franchise opportunities are open in several regions.
