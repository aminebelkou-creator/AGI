Admissions

Tours are scheduled individually, so call for an appointment to meet us and see the school. Every new student sits placement assessments before admission — whatever system they arrive from — and registration for the coming year takes place on August 28 and 31.

The application journey

01

Enquire

Call or email the admissions team to arrange a visit; tours are individual and include the school and the application process.

02

Visit & Assess

English/ESL proficiency and academic assessments, including the free Anglia placement test for language learners.

03

Apply

Prior school records and a behaviour report, age and residency verification, immunisation and physical examination records.

04

Enroll

Pay the annual inscription, confirm the monthly tuition plan, and order books from the beginning of August.

Tuition & Fees

2,600 MAD per month, paid over ten months.

Plus one annual inscription at enrolment: 6,500 MAD for Kindergarten–Grade 1, rising to 11,000 MAD for Grade 12. Books, mathematics and Anglia English examinations, dual enrolment in the USA and Morocco, and student insurance are included. Inscription is non-refundable; fees are not negotiable.

What to bring

A child aged 5 by September 1 and under 21 on the first day of school is eligible for enrolment. AGI admits students of any race, religion, colour, national or ethnic origin.

Age

Birth certificate, hospital or physician certificate, passport or visa, or birth registration.

Residency

Lease or mortgage statement, utility bill, tax statement or deed, or a job contract — dated within three months.

Health

Immunisation certification and a physical examination record.

Academic

Records from the previous school (or its address), plus a behaviour report. IEP or special-needs provision requires practitioner documentation.
