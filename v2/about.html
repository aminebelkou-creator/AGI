The first American school in Agadir.

AGI began in 2013 with five children in one room. Today it is a K–12 micro-school, a center for adult and English education, and the home of Anglia Examinations in Morocco.

Our story

American Group International School is a small private school in the Berber community of the Souss, in Agadir. The first classes started in 2013 with five children. The school soon outgrew its one-room beginnings and moved to Hay Nahda, where kindergarten to grade 12 is now taught in eight multi-grade classrooms.

Over eleven years, AGI has welcomed students of more than 46 nationalities. Its enrollment is open: the school was created for students who need a program the Moroccan system may not offer – an American curriculum, English immersion, or support for learning differences.

In 2021 AGI became the Anglia Examinations franchise holder for Morocco, and it now runs the Anglia network across the Kingdom's twelve regions from its Agadir headquarters. Its mission remains the same: an international education that working families in Morocco can afford.

Mission, philosophy and vision

Mission

To serve the Agadir region by giving children a strong educational foundation following American philosophy and practice, so that they become independent, critical thinkers and caring, contributing members of society.

Philosophy

Academic excellence, discipline, ethical standards and respect for others. We develop the whole child – intellectual, social, emotional, character and physical – and foster a general respect for all humanity.

Vision

Lifelong learners who pursue excellence, model integrity and honor cultural diversity, equipped to serve their community in Morocco and the wider world with wisdom and compassion.

Leadership

Julie A. Wilburn

CEO & Educational Director · Anglia Examinations Morocco, American Group International of Agadir

Julie Wilburn leads all of AGI's educational programs: the primary and high school, curriculum, testing and assessment, staff training and the social-skills program. She also teaches in the classroom.

She has lived in Morocco for many years and holds dual citizenship. In 2026 she was accredited as a Holistic Therapeutic Coach by IPHM and ICAHP, and she offers ADHD holistic coaching for children and adults.

Accreditation & memberships

Where AGI's standards come from, and who holds us to them.

American Council of Training and DevelopmentAccreditation
AGI's institutional accreditor for the American-curriculum school and its programs.
U.S. state departments of educationRecognition
Recognized by the departments of education of Tennessee, Florida, Colorado and Alabama.
CogniaAccreditation
The American High School Online program for grades 9–12 is Cognia accredited; the high school books program is Florida accredited.
QAHEAccreditation
Quality Assurance in Higher Education covers AGI's independent course studies and professional programs.
American Canadian BoardCertification
Professional training certificates, including the Early Childhood Education and Care diploma, are approved by the American Canadian Board.
AIM Qualifications · OfqualRegulation
Anglia's English qualifications are delivered with AIM Qualifications and Assessment Group, regulated by Ofqual in England.
UCASRecognition
The Anglia Proficiency examination is listed by UCAS as evidence of English competence for British universities.
Global Schools ProgramMembership
An initiative of the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network, supporting education for sustainable development. Member since 2023.
American School Counselor AssociationMembership
AGI joined ASCA in 2026, bringing U.S. school-counseling standards to student guidance.
