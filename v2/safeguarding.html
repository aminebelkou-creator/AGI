Safeguarding & child protection.

Every child at AGI has the right to be safe, respected and heard. This page explains how the school protects children and how to raise a concern.

Raise a concernAll policies

Our commitments

  • Children's safety and wellbeing come first in every decision.
  • Staff know the children and families they work with, and listen when a child speaks up.
  • Concerns are taken seriously, recorded and acted on promptly.
  • We work with parents and, where needed, with the competent authorities.

Who is responsible

Designated safeguarding lead: name and role to be confirmed. Overall responsibility: Julie A. Wilburn, Founder · CEO & Educational Director.

Photos, video and children's names

No child's photo, video or full name is published without written parental consent, in line with our media policy and Morocco's personal-data law (09-08). On this website, photos of children are hidden until that consent is confirmed.

Online safety

Students use school devices and online classes under the acceptable-use rules in the academic handbook. Online lessons run on Zoom or Microsoft Teams with the school's instructions.

Wellbeing and support

Individual plans, small classes and holistic support help children who are struggling. AGI is a member of the American School Counselor Association. Wellbeing (i-Heart) · Special education

Visitors

Visits to the school are by appointment and arranged individually. Visitor sign-in and supervision procedure to be confirmed.

How to raise a concern

If you are worried about a child's safety or wellbeing, speak to the school office or the safeguarding lead straight away – in person, by phone on +212 528 843 349 or by email at americanecoleagadir@gmail.com. You can also use the complaints procedure. If a child is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services first.

Children's personal data

We collect only the information needed to educate and protect each child, keep it confidential and share it only with people who need it. Privacy policy
